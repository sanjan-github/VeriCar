          Indian Market Document Verification Checklist

          01. RC & Parivahan NOC

          Verify original RC smart card, match physical chassis stamp under hood against RC, and check transfer clearance on Parivahan Sewa.

          02. Insurance & NCB History

          Ensure continuous insurance cover and examine No Claim Bonus (NCB) records to uncover undisclosed accident repairs.

          03. Hypothecation (HP) Removal

          Verify Form 35 and Bank NOC to ensure active loan liens are formally removed from the registration certificate.

          04. Service Invoices & PUC

          Examine stamped authorized workshop invoices to cross-verify odometer mileage progression and valid emissions certificate.
